Batra Super Speciality Hospital

Instagram grid directions, diagnostics posts and 4-slide carousels, making a hospital feed feel less clinical.

Client
Batra Super Speciality Hospital, Sonipat
My role
Social media design
What I made
Grid directions, carousels, post concepts
Tools
Canva, Claude, ChatGPT

The brief

A hospital feed has a hard job: it has to be trusted, and it also has to be something people don't scroll straight past. The brief was to plan and design Instagram content that felt credible without looking like a pamphlet.

What I made

  • Several full-grid directions so the team could pick a look before we made individual posts, from a premium red-and-gold route to a softer, more aesthetic one.
  • A content grid built around nine pillars, so posting wasn't just "whatever we have this week".
  • A diagnostics-focused grid, plus two 4-slide carousels that explain things step by step.
  • Individual post concepts the team could reuse as templates.
What I learned

Showing a client three directions side by side gets a faster, clearer answer than asking "what do you like?"
