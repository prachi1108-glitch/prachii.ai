Discount-coupon creatives designed to be read in two seconds on a phone.

Client
Malwa Hyundai
My role
Campaign creatives
What I made
Discount coupon designs
Tools
Canva, Claude

The brief

A car dealership running an offer needs one thing from a creative: the offer has to be understood instantly, especially on a small screen.

What I made

  • Coupon-style creatives with the discount as the loudest thing on the page.
  • Layouts that still work when cropped for different placements.
What I learned

If someone has to read it twice, it has already failed. Cut until the offer is the only thing left.
