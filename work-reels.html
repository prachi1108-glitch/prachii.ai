My own reels

Design-tips reels that reached 18K and 9K views, and taught me that reach isn't the same as a brand.

Client
My own Instagram
My role
Creator
What I made
Short-form reels
Tools
Canva, CapCut

The brief

I started posting quick design tips because they were easy to make and people watched them. Two of them reached 18K and 9K views.

What I made

  • Short, hook-first reels showing one design trick at a time.
  • Tried different hooks and formats to see what made people stop scrolling.
What I learned

What gets views isn't always what builds a brand. I don't want to be "the Canva girl". I want people to follow me for how I think.
