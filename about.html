About

Hi, I'm Prachi. I'm figuring it out.

I'm 18. I finished school and chose to build my own path instead of picking one because everyone else did.

The short version

Everyone around me seemed to know what came next: college, engineering, medicine, an exam. I didn't, and I stopped pretending that I did.

So I started learning the skills I was curious about (digital marketing, AI, content and storytelling) by using them on real projects, for real brands, and posting what I learned along the way.

I'm not an expert, and this site isn't pretending I am. It's a record of what I'm trying, what worked, and what didn't.

What I believe

  • Clarity comes from doing things, not from thinking about them forever.
  • Tools like Canva and AI are useful, but they're not the point. Ideas are.
  • Documenting the messy middle is more useful than only posting the wins.
