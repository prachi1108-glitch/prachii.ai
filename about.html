About

Hi, I'm Prachi. Creator first, marketer always.

I create content, study what makes people stop and share, and use what I learn to help brands tell better stories.

The story

I've always been the person who notices why something works.

Why one reel gets shared and another gets skipped. Why one caption makes you stop, and the next one doesn't. For a long time that was just curiosity. Then I decided to do something with it.

Alongside my degree, I started creating. Not because a syllabus asked me to, but because the fastest way to understand content is to make it, post it, and watch what happens. My own Instagram became my lab: new hooks, new formats, a 30-day daily reel challenge, and plenty of posts that taught me more by not working.

Creating taught me what an audience feels. Marketing taught me what a brand needs. I like working where the two meet.

That's what I bring to every project, whether it's a doctor explaining things patients are nervous to ask, a local business finding its voice, or my own channel: understand the people first, then decide what's worth saying.

I use AI every day, as a thinking partner and as a builder. I built this website with it, including the version of me that's been following your cursor. But the part I care about most is still the human part: knowing an audience well enough to say the one thing they needed to hear.

How I work

  • Audience first. Before I write a post, I write down who it's for and the question they're asking.
  • Every post has a job. Earn trust, answer a question or get an enquiry. If it has no job, it doesn't go out.
  • Speak their language. Literally. Hinglish, when that's how the audience talks at home.
  • Measure, then adjust. What people save, share and ask about decides what I make next.
