Vardaan

Logo concepts and a website build for a farm-fresh egg brand.

Client
Vardaan
My role
Brand concepts & website
What I made
Logo concepts, multi-page website
Tools
Claude, Next.js, Payload CMS

The brief

Vardaan sells eggs, to homes and in bulk to businesses. It needed a brand that felt fresh and trustworthy, and a website that could take wholesale enquiries.

What I made

  • A set of logo concepts exploring different moods for the brand.
  • A website with pages for products, the story behind the farm, a blog and a wholesale enquiry form.
  • A simple admin panel so product and blog content can be updated without touching code.
What I learned

A website is mostly decisions about what a visitor needs next. The design comes after that.
