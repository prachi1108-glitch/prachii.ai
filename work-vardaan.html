Vardaan

Logo concepts and a website for a farm-fresh egg brand that sells to homes and in bulk to businesses.

Client
Vardaan
My role
Brand concepts & website
What I made
Logo concepts, multi-page website
Tools
Claude, Next.js, Payload CMS

The brief

Vardaan sells eggs, to homes and in bulk to businesses. It needed a brand that felt fresh and trustworthy, and a website that could turn visitors into wholesale enquiries.

My approach

I started from the two people the site has to serve: a family buying eggs, and a business owner buying by the tray. Everything on the site had to make one of them trust the farm faster.

What I did

  • A set of logo concepts exploring different directions for the brand.
  • A website with pages for products, the story behind the farm, a blog and a wholesale enquiry form.
  • A simple admin panel so products and blog posts can be updated without touching code.
What I learned

A website is mostly decisions about what a visitor needs next. The design comes after that.
