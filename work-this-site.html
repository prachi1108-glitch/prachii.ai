This website

An illustrated version of me that turns to follow your cursor, built with AI tools.

Client
Me
My role
Concept, design direction & build
What I made
Portfolio website with an interactive hero
Tools
Antigravity, Claude, GitHub, Vercel

The brief

I wanted a portfolio that people would remember, not a template.

My approach

The idea was simple: if a visitor looks at my work, my portfolio should look back at them.

What I did

  • Created a short animation of my character turning her head, then split it into 64 frames, one for every direction.
  • Built a script that picks the right frame based on where your cursor is, so she always looks at you.
  • Published it with GitHub and Vercel, and debugged the first broken deploy myself.
What I learned

Shipping something, breaking it and fixing it teaches you more than planning it perfectly.
