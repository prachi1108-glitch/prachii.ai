An illustrated me that turns to follow your cursor, built with AI tools and a lot of trial and error.

What I made

Tools
Antigravity, Claude, GitHub, Vercel

The brief

I wanted a portfolio that felt like me, not a template. So I made an illustrated version of myself that follows your cursor.

What I made

  • Generated a short animation of my character turning her head, then split it into 64 frames, one for every direction.
  • Wrote a script that picks the right frame based on where your cursor is, so she always looks at you.
  • Put it on GitHub and hosted it on Vercel. The first deploy was a black screen, and fixing it taught me more than the build did.
What I learned

Shipping the thing, breaking it, and fixing it in public teaches you more than planning it perfectly.
