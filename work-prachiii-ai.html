The brief

Before I touched a client's account, I tested ideas on my own. My channel is where I try new formats, hooks and topics, and see what an audience actually responds to.

My approach

I started with quick Canva tutorials because they were easy to make and people watched them. Two of them reached 18K and 9K views. The numbers taught me something more useful: reach and reputation are different goals. So I moved towards content that shows how I think, like documenting how I built this website with AI.

What I did

  • Hook-first design tutorials, one technique per reel.
  • Tested formats, openings and lengths to see what made people stop scrolling.
  • A building-in-public series about working with AI tools like Claude.
What I learned

What gets views and what builds a brand are two different things. I now plan content for the second.
