The brief

Before I touched a client's account, I tested ideas on my own. My channel is where I try new formats, hooks and topics, and see what an audience actually responds to, with my own numbers on the line.

My approach

I ran a 30-day challenge: one design reel every day, each teaching a single Canva technique in under a minute. Posting daily forced me to test hooks fast and keep what worked. Day 28 broke out to 3.1 lakh views, and 94% of my views now come from people who don't follow me yet, which means the content is travelling on its own. Next, I'm moving from tutorials towards content that shows how I think, like documenting how I built this website with AI.

What I did

  • Planned and posted a 30-day daily reel series, one technique per reel.
  • Wrote hook-first openings in English and Hinglish, and tested lengths and formats.
  • Tracked views, shares and follows in Insights to decide what to make next.
  • Started a building-in-public series about working with AI tools like Claude.
